### 3. HOD Demonstration & Feedback Summary
On **July 23, 2026**, the development team presented a live demonstration of the CCMS mobile application to the Head of the Department (HOD) of Computer Science & Engineering.

#### Key Feedback & Discussions:
1.  **General Evaluation:** The HOD commended the application's user interface, navigation, and the automated weekly syllabus completion reports, noting it would significantly save time for class coordinators.
2.  **App Distribution & Accessibility:**  
    *   *Query:* The HOD inquired about the distribution model and how faculty members would download the application.
    *   *Resolution:* The team explained that the application will be hosted on the **Google Play Store** (for Android users) and the **Apple App Store** (for iOS users) under a college developer profile. This ensures safe, verified, and easy installation for all faculty members.
3.  **Security & Domain Restriction:**  
    *   *Action Item:* The HOD requested that registration and login be strictly restricted to the official college email domain (**`@jec.com`**) to prevent unauthorized access.
    *   *Status:* The development team is currently implementing this restriction in the authentication service, scheduled to go live by **July 29, 2026**.
